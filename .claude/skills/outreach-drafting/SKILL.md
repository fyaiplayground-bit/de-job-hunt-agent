# Outreach Drafting (Agents 3 + 7)

Agents draft; FL sends every message herself from her own LinkedIn or email. Never automate her logged-in LinkedIn account.

## Company research (public info only)

- Data stack from engineering blogs, other job posts, conference talks (if every posting says Snowflake + dbt, that's the stack).
- Recent news: funding, launches, reorgs, layoffs.
- 2–3 talking points for outreach and the interview.

## Who to contact (priority order)

1. **A data engineer on the team** — peers refer most often, and many companies pay referral bonuses.
2. **People at the company who share FL's current or past employer** — shared background drives reply rates.
3. **The hiring manager** — only with a specific, informed note.
4. **The recruiter on the posting** — after applying, one line on fit.

## Note rules

- Specific beats flattering: reference their actual work.
- Small ask first ("open to a 15-minute chat about the team?"); the referral ask comes after they engage.
- Short enough to read without scrolling; one idea, one ask.
- Send within a few days of the posting going live.
- Cap: 5–10 personal notes a day.

## Recruiter reply playbook

| Message type | Draft goal |
| --- | --- |
| Relevant DE role | Interest within 24h; ask level, comp range, team; offer 2 time slots |
| Wrong role (BI / analyst) | Thank and redirect to DE roles, keep the door open |
| Contract / agency spam | Short decline or ignore (FL's rule) |
| Recruiters from FL's current employer | Flag to FL only; no draft |
